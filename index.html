Three ways to listen

Every voice in BookSpeaks is AI speech, not a human narrator. Here is how they sound.

  1. 1 of 3

    Offline on your phone

    Offline voices run on your iPhone itself: no connection, no credits. The first hour is free; a single $14.99 purchase makes it unlimited, forever.

  2. 2 of 3

    Generated on our server

    Have a whole book voiced ahead of time for a few credits.

    The finished audiobook stays on your phone and plays with no internet.

  3. 3 of 3 · Highest quality

    Streamed as you listen

    The highest quality we have: the book is voiced on our server and streamed as you listen. It needs a connection and is paid in credits.

Plain answers

Which books can I open?
EPUB, FB2 and TXT files without DRM. Books bought with copy protection cannot be opened. BookSpeaks does not sell books.
Do I need internet?
Only for streamed AI voices and while an audiobook is being pre-generated. Offline voices and finished audiobooks play with no connection.
Is there a subscription?
No. Offline listening is a single $14.99 purchase, and credits are bought in packs when you want them.
Are the voices human?
No. Every voice is AI speech, not a recording of a human narrator.
