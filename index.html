No subscription · No account · No ads

Inside the app

A library of your own files, a player that stays out of the way, and a list of voices to choose from.

Three ways to listen

Every voice in BookSpeaks is AI speech, not a human narrator. You choose where it is made.

  1. No internet

    Offline voices on the phone

    The voice runs on your iPhone itself, so it needs no connection: it works on a plane, in the metro or far from any signal.

  2. Streamed · paid in credits

    Natural AI voices from our server

    For a more natural sound, the book is voiced on our server and streamed to you as you listen. This needs a connection and spends credits.

  3. Made once · then offline

    A pre-generated audiobook

    Our server voices the whole book in advance, for credits. After that the audiobook is on your phone and plays without internet.

Bring your own books

Import the EPUB, FB2 or TXT files you already have, as long as they have no DRM. Read them on screen or listen to them — reading is free.

BookSpeaks does not sell books. There is no account to create and there are no ads.

Plain answers

Which books can I open?
EPUB, FB2 and TXT files without DRM. Books bought with copy protection cannot be opened. BookSpeaks does not sell books.
Do I need internet?
Only for streamed AI voices and while an audiobook is being pre-generated. Offline voices and finished audiobooks play with no connection.
Is there a subscription?
No. Offline listening is a single $14.99 purchase, and credits are bought in packs when you want them.
Are the voices human?
No. Every voice is AI speech, not a recording of a human narrator.
