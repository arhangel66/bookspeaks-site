Your books, read aloud

Turn your own EPUB, FB2 and TXT books into an audiobook — and read them too.

Download on the App Store

Listen with voices on your iPhone

Download an AI voice once and it reads on the device, offline. Until then the built-in Apple voice reads. Playback speed, sleep timer and lock-screen controls.

Read, and keep your place

Switch between Read and Listen at any time. Paged reading in light and dark styles, with progress and time left for every book.

Send a book by mail or AirDrop

BookSpeaks gives you an address of your own: mail a book to it from any mailbox and it appears in your library. Or add it from Files, AirDrop or «Open in».

No account and no ads. BookSpeaks does not sell books: it reads the files you already have. Books with DRM cannot be opened.
